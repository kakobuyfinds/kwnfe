Field note 01 / Footwear

A sneaker shortlist needs more than a size label.

For a Nike sneaker size comparison, begin with the specific model and the seller's measurement method. An EU shoe size conversion is only a starting point: the same label can feel different across shapes.

  • Foot length measurement: measure both feet while standing. Use the larger measurement and compare it with the stated chart method.
  • Sneaker insole length check: ask whether the measurement refers to the foot, removable insole or internal shoe space. These are different values.
  • Wide foot sneaker fit: look for width and toe-box information; choosing a longer shoe does not necessarily solve a narrow fit.
  • Sneaker outsole photos: inspect tread condition and sole edges. For a shoe colorway comparison, ask for consistent lighting across both shoes.

Keep the exact source link and selected color together in your sneaker spreadsheet. If a photo shows several versions, confirm which version the seller is offering.

Field note 02 / Clothing & accessories

Build a clothing size guide from clothes you own.

Lay a well-fitting garment flat and measure it using the seller's method. An oversized hoodie measurement guide should compare chest width, shoulder width and body length; a relaxed fit should still suit your proportions.

Field note 03 / Photo evidence

A warehouse QC checklist should answer specific questions.

Begin a warehouse photo inspection by matching the order's item, size and color to the photos. Then look for a visible defect check: uneven seams, stains, damaged closures or mismatched parts.

For a clothing stitching close-up, request the seam that concerns you. For a product measurement photo, the tape's start and end should both be visible. A seller photo versus QC photo comparison can reveal differences in the received item, though lighting can change perceived color.

Before accepting an item: record unresolved questions, check the current return eligibility and deadline, and ask for relevant detail photos. QC can document visible condition; it cannot authenticate a brand or confirm every material claim.

Field note 04 / Costs & destination

Plan the parcel before the shortlist grows.

A useful total landed cost checklist includes the item, domestic delivery, service charges, payment conversion, international freight and any destination import charges. Keep estimated amounts separate from confirmed charges.

For parcel weight versus volume, request the packed measurements: a bulky box may be priced differently from a compact parcel of the same weight. A shipping estimate before ordering remains an estimate until packaging and route details are known.

Compare quotes for your actual destination and currency, whether you are researching from the US, Canada, the UK or the EU. Check the selected carrier's restrictions and the destination customs authority's current requirements. A return policy before purchase check should also cover who pays return delivery and whether service charges are refundable.

Source link availability check: reopen each saved listing before paying. If it has disappeared, treat the row as a research reference rather than evidence that the item can still be ordered.

Direct answers

Before you follow a find.

What is a SuperBuy spreadsheet?

It is a product research list that typically groups seller links, photos and item notes. Its contents depend on the compiler; a row does not guarantee stock, authenticity or compatibility with a buying service.

Where do the browsing links on this page go?

The category links open the matching HipobuyFind categories. All other browsing links open the HipobuyFind homepage. This page does not provide Superbuy checkout or a downloadable spreadsheet.

Can QC photos prove that an item is authentic?

No. QC photos can help you inspect visible condition, measurements and the selected option. They do not establish provenance, material composition or brand authenticity.

How should I choose a clothing size?

Measure a comparable garment that fits, then compare those measurements with the seller's chart. Check the units, measurement method and stated tolerance rather than relying on a letter size.

Does a listed item price include delivery and taxes?

Do not assume it does. Check the item cost, domestic delivery, service and payment charges, international freight and destination import charges separately before committing.

Is this an official Superbuy or fashion brand website?

No. The Find Edit is an independent informational page with no claimed affiliation to Superbuy or the brands mentioned. Brand names and catalog photos identify research subjects, not verified authentic merchandise.
